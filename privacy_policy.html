Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EDS Work Order Manager handles information when authorized users access and use the application.

EDS Work Order Manager is an internal business application intended for authorized EDS staff and operational users only.

1. Information We Process

The app may process information required to provide work order and operational management functions, including:

  • User login or authentication information.
  • User ID, staff, or technician identification information.
  • Work order records, work order status, remarks, and related job details.
  • Part request information, approval status, and on-hand or disposal status.
  • Basic technical information needed for app functionality, such as network access and session state.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only for business and app functionality purposes, including:

  • Authenticating authorized users.
  • Displaying and managing work orders.
  • Supporting technician coordination and operational workflows.
  • Managing part request approval, tracking, and disposal actions.
  • Maintaining app security, reliability, and internal business records.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Information handled by the app is used for internal EDS operational purposes and may be processed by company backend systems or service providers required to operate the application.

4. Data Security

Access to the app is restricted to authorized users. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

5. Data Retention

Operational data is retained for as long as necessary to support work order management, internal business needs, legal requirements, audit requirements, or company record-keeping policies.

6. User Access

Because this is an internal business application, access is managed by EDS or authorized administrators. Users who need assistance with access, account details, or data questions should contact the company administrator or support contact listed below.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children and is not directed to users under the age of 13. The app is intended only for authorized business users.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
