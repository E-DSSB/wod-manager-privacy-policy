Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when authorized users access and use EDS Work Order Manager. The app is provided for internal business operations, work order monitoring, technician coordination, and related service-management activities.

EDS Work Order Manager is intended only for authorized EDS personnel, administrators, technicians, and approved operational users. The app is not intended for public consumer use.

1. Scope Of This Policy

This policy applies to the EDS Work Order Manager mobile application and related services used to support work order and part request workflows. It explains the types of information processed, why the information is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the user's role and the functions used, the app may process the following categories of information:

  • Account and authentication information, such as user IDs, login credentials, and session information.
  • Staff, technician, or operational user information required for job assignment and identification.
  • Work order information, including job details, statuses, customer or site references, remarks, dates, and related service records.
  • Part request information, including requested parts, approval status, on-hand status, disposal status, and related operational actions.
  • Technical information required to operate the app, such as device, network, and application session information.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed only for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Authenticating authorized users and protecting access to the app.
  • Displaying, updating, and managing work orders and service activities.
  • Supporting technician coordination, job tracking, and internal operational workflows.
  • Managing part request approvals, availability, on-hand records, and disposal actions.
  • Maintaining security, auditability, reliability, and continuity of the service.
  • Complying with applicable business, legal, accounting, audit, or record-keeping requirements.

4. Data Sharing And Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information handled by the app is used for internal EDS operational purposes. Information may be made available to authorized internal users, administrators, backend systems, hosting providers, or service providers only where required to operate, secure, maintain, or support the app and related business processes.

5. Data Security

Access to the app is restricted to authorized users. Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse. Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials secure and for using the app only for approved business purposes.

6. Data Retention

Operational records are retained for as long as necessary to support work order management, business operations, legal obligations, audit requirements, dispute resolution, and company record-keeping policies. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and applicable business needs.

7. User Access And Account Management

Access to EDS Work Order Manager is managed by EDS or authorized administrators. Users who require assistance with access, account details, corrections, or data-related questions should contact the administrator or support contact listed below.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is designed for business use and is not directed to children. It is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13.

9. Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or technical changes. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
